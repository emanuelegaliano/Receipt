What it does

Everything Stays in Your Pocket

A complete expense tracker for card and cash: receipt OCR, categories that learn, monthly limits and backups. All on the device.

RapidOCR · PP-OCRv4 ONNX Runtime

On-Device Receipt OCR

The RapidOCR pipeline (detection, orientation, recognition) is ported to Dart and runs with ONNX Runtime on the phone. The PyReceipt parser then finds the total, the date and the shop, and reads from the receipt whether you paid by card or in cash. A scan takes about 3 seconds on a recent phone.

How Receipt Works in Action

Screenshots from the Android emulator, with made-up data.

1 Scan a receipt

Take a Photo, Check, Save

Photograph a receipt or pick one from the gallery. A few seconds later the form is filled in: amount, shop, date, suggested category and payment method. You check it and save.

  • The category comes with its reason, e.g. suggested by the word “trattoria” on the receipt.
  • Card or cash is detected from the payment lines (“pagamento contante”, “elettronico”).
  • Manual entry for expenses without a receipt, with shop autocomplete.
2 Monthly summary

Your Month at a Glance

The total for the month, the change from the previous one and the split between card and cash. Under it, the last six months as columns: tap one to jump to that month.

  • Accounts are yours to define: card, cash, a prepaid card…
  • Movements grouped by day, with search, filters and swipe-to-delete with undo.
3 Monthly limits

Know When You Hit a Limit

Give a category a monthly limit, say €100 for leisure. When an expense takes you over it, Receipt tells you right away: “This month you spent €120.00 against a limit of €100.00: €20.00 over.”

  • A note at 80%, a reminder for every expense once you are over.
  • Limits warn, they never block: every expense is still recorded.
  • Each limit then appears as a marker on its category bar.
4 Per category

Every Category, Readable at a Glance

One row per category, all on the same scale: the longer the bar, the more you spent. Each row packs the whole story into a few marks.

The bar is what you spent this month, with the amount on the right.
The dot marks where you were last month.
The tick is your monthly limit; the bar turns amber at 80% and red past it.
The line below spells it out: “+€71.56 vs September · €22.04 left”.
5 Yours only

Locked, Private, Your Colours

Receipt locks itself when you leave it, and opens with your password or fingerprint. Choose light, dark or system theme and one of six accent colours.

  • Only a salted PBKDF2 hash of the password is stored, in the Android Keystore.
  • No lock while you are taking a photo or sharing a backup.

Where it comes from

From the original idea of PyReceipt

Receipt brings PyReceipt — a lightweight Python receipt reader with a Telegram assistant — to Android. The OCR pipeline and the receipt parser were ported to Dart, with fixes for tilted photos, shop names and Italian receipts.
