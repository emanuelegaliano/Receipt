# Ideas and to-dos

## Ideas
- **Recurring payments**: expenses that repeat (rent, subscriptions, bills),
  added automatically every month or week.
- **Year in review**: a recap at the end of the year, in the style of Spotify
  Wrapped, computed on the phone: months within the limits, the category where
  spending dropped the most compared with the previous year, the most frequent
  shop, the biggest expense, how many receipts were scanned, and at the end the
  year's island with the reward at its centre. Shareable as an image, without
  amounts. Comes after the island.
